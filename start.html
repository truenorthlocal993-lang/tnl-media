Your free 30-Day Beginner Plan

New to the gym? You've got this. One small step a day, no experience needed. Follow along with @builtdifferentdaily_1 on Instagram.

Week 1: Just show up

  • Walk 20 minutes every day. That's the whole goal.
  • 2 easy full-body sessions: 2 rounds of 10 bodyweight squats, 8 push-ups (on your knees is fine), 10 glute bridges, a 20-second plank.
  • Drink a glass of water with every meal.

Week 2: Build the habit

  • Walk 25 minutes a day.
  • 3 full-body sessions: add light dumbbells to the squats and do 10 dumbbell rows per side.
  • Add a palm-sized serving of protein to each meal.

Week 3: Learn the moves

  • Walk 30 minutes a day, or swap one day for a bike ride or swim.
  • 3 sessions, 3 rounds each. Focus on slow, controlled reps and good form.
  • Aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Recovery is where progress happens.

Week 4: Own it

  • Keep the daily walk. It's yours now.
  • 3 sessions: add one more exercise you enjoy (lunges, overhead press or a bike sprint).
  • Look back at day 1. Write down one thing that feels easier now. Then start month 2.

Coach's rules

  • Missed a day? Just do the next one. No guilt.
  • Go light and learn the moves first. Heavy comes later.
  • Celebrate every win, even small ones.

General fitness information, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new program, and stop any exercise that causes pain.
