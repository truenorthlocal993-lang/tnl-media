Monthly posting

Three taps and you’re done.

For Monthly posting clients. We do the setup; you just approve. Your page stays yours and nothing posts without your OK.

  1. Tap OrderChoose Monthly posting in the pricing table and how many posts you want.
  2. Tap Approve on our access requestWe send a request from our Meta business account to your Facebook Page and Instagram. You get a notification or email from Meta: open it and tap Approve. No passwords, and you can remove us any time.
  3. Tap Approve on your postsEach month we send a preview link with 3 to 4 new posts made from your work. One tap to approve, or reply with changes. Meta's own scheduler publishes them on your page.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We set it up and keep it running. You approve the wording once and get on with your work.

What does a website cost?

A one-time build fee, with optional monthly hosting and small edits. You own the site and its content.

What if my business isn't a trade?

Not at all. Salons, restaurants, shops, studios, gyms, clinics and trades all need the same thing: a clear site and ads that show real work. Tell us about yours and we'll say honestly if we're a fit.

How do you post on my page?

Three taps: order, approve our access request from Meta (no passwords), then approve each month's posts. We do everything else and Meta publishes them on schedule.

Why don't you show reviews?

We're new and we won't invent them. Real client results will go here once clients agree to share them.
